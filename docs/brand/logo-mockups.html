Gino: two logo directions

Mock-ups drawn from the two logotypes. Each pairs a wordmark with a G app icon in the same hand.

A · 1950s print

The script "Gino", printed in cream ink on dark board. Rough edges and specks where the ink didn't take, like a worn letterpress matchbook.

B · Rounded

The chunky marker-drawn GINO: thick round strokes with a slight wobble, black on white. The icon is its G on warm paper.
